## What Colombia built that no indicator counts

These are documented institutional changes in Colombia that the framework records as evidence. They appear beside the score and do not change it or its confidence.

- **4G road concessions, a national infrastructure portfolio** (Building). By September 2024, Colombia's 4G road programme comprised 30 concessions at 86.99% aggregate progress, with 14 in operation, 13 under construction, two in pre-construction and one in early termination.
- **SECOP II, a transaction platform for public procurement** (Coordination). Since March 2015, Colombia Compra Eficiente has used SECOP II to move public contracting from publication to online transactions; in 2024 the platform managed COP117.642 trillion, 84.4% of the value managed across the three SECOP platforms.
- **Cédula digital, an identity service upgraded for mobile use** (Adaptability). In 2024 Colombia's Registraduría deployed version 2.0 of its digital ID app and sent 2,642,775 polycarbonate digital identity cards to offices nationwide while continuing a pilot to refine digital services.
- **Victims' reparations, a long-running administrative response to conflict** (Adaptability). In 2024 Colombia's Unidad para las Víctimas ordered 307,619 administrative indemnifications benefiting 285,537 victims, approved 79 collective reparation plans and closed three plans for communities affected by the armed conflict.
- **Fondo Adaptación, recovery and safer resettlement after La Niña** (Adaptability). After the 2010–11 La Niña floods, Colombia's Fondo Adaptación had advanced resettlement, reconstruction and improvement for more than 39,000 households by its 2021–23 evaluation, presenting the work as reducing climate exposure and fiscal risk.
- **Reficar, a refinery modernization with a fiscal-liability finding** (Building). A 2021 Contraloría fiscal-responsibility ruling on Colombia's Reficar modernization held COP2.945 trillion in damage after finding USD997 million in additional investments that added no value to the project.
- **SENA complementary training (formación complementaria)** (Learning). Colombia's Servicio Nacional de Aprendizaje, created by decree in 1957, recorded 5,469,951 learners in short complementary training courses in 2023 (7,035,749 course places), and certified 3,661,218 completions in that strand the same year.
