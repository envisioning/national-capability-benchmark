## O que o Reino Unido construiu e nenhum indicador conta

Estas são mudanças institucionais documentadas em Reino Unido que o framework registra como evidência. Elas aparecem ao lado da nota e não mudam a nota nem a solidez da evidência.

- **Sure Start children's centres, and their erosion** (Construção). England built a national network of early-childhood centres reaching 3,632 sites by August 2009, then closed or hollowed out a large part of it after funding was cut, with the official count at 3,123 by October 2017 and independent estimates near 1,000 closures.
- **The FCA regulatory sandbox** (Experimentação). The United Kingdom's financial regulator built the first regulatory sandbox in 2016, and by the end of 2022 had accepted 168 firms and products to test with real customers under supervision.
- **Legally binding carbon budgets** (Antecipação). The United Kingdom's 2008 Climate Change Act made five-year emissions budgets a standing legal cycle set twelve years ahead, and by July 2026 seven budgets covered 2008 through 2042 while the first three had been met.
- **Knowledge Transfer Partnerships** (Coordenação). UKRI reported more than 1,000 Knowledge Transfer Partnerships running at one time and more than 1,100 Associate projects in May 2026, joining businesses with universities or other research organisations through jointly supervised innovation work.
- **British Patient Capital, a public venture-capital catalyst** (Experimentação). The British Business Bank reported that government-owned British Patient Capital had committed £2.287 billion through 80 fund commitments, 10 co-investments and 18 Future Fund deals by 31 March 2024, with an underlying portfolio of 1,350 companies.
- **ICURe, from university research to spinouts** (Experimentação). UK Research and Innovation's ICURe impact report says spinout companies from the programme secured £326 million in investment funding and created more than 1,400 jobs from 2014 onward, after research teams were trained and funded to test markets before deciding whether to spin out.
- **Business-funded R&D as the UK's dominant research sector** (Experimentação). The Office for National Statistics reported that UK businesses performed £50.0 billion of R&D in 2023, 69% of the country's £72.6 billion gross domestic R&D total.
- **High Speed Two, and the cancellation of Phase 2** (Construção). The Department for Transport and HS2 Ltd began building High Speed Two in 2020. The government cancelled everything beyond Birmingham in 2023. By May 2026 the London to Birmingham railway alone was estimated at £87.7 billion to £102.7 billion, more than double the 2020 Phase 1 estimate, with services from Old Oak Common expected between 2036 and 2039 against a 2012 plan to complete the full network by 2033.
- **Crossrail, the Elizabeth line** (Construção). The Department for Transport and Transport for London built Crossrail through Crossrail Ltd from 2009. The NAO put its forecast cost at 28% above the 2010 budget of £14.8 billion and its opening at up to three and a half years past the original December 2018 date. The central section opened in 2022 and carried 257.4 million journeys between April 2025 and March 2026.
- **COVID-19 Vaccine Taskforce and national vaccination rollout** (Adaptação). The UK government set up the Vaccine Taskforce in April 2020, became the first country to authorise a COVID-19 vaccine and begin vaccination in December 2020, and by the end of October 2021 the NHS in England had given two doses to 85% of adults.
