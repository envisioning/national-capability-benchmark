## What Paraguay built that no indicator counts

These are documented institutional changes in Paraguay that the framework files as evidence. They sit beside the score and do not change it or its confidence.

- **Tekoporã Mbarete family-support programme** (Adaptability). Paraguay's Tekoporã Mbarete social-protection programme protected more than 175,000 families nationwide in 2024, paid monthly after a 2023 redesign and reported 95% bankarisation plus georeferencing of 11,316 participating homes.
- **Itaipú binational hydropower operations** (Building). Itaipú supplied Paraguay's grid with 20,383 GWh in 2024, 4.5% more than in 2023, while the binational plant generated 67,089 GWh in total and continued operating after four decades of service.
- **SIFEN national electronic invoicing rollout** (Adaptability). Paraguay's tax administration approved 651,894,711 electronic tax documents during 2024 and reported 6,659 electronic billers using the SIFEN system, combining mandatory onboarding for larger firms with the free e-Kuatia'i route for small taxpayers.
- **Ruta PY02 public-private road concession** (Coordination). Paraguay's public-works balance records the Ruta PY02 duplication and expansion as a 149.5-kilometre public-private partnership, awarded under the 2013 APP law with a USD 507 million construction contract and a concession running to 2046.
- **Metrobús, an unfinished bus rapid transit project** (Building). Paraguay's planned Asunción Metrobús corridor ended with an incomplete and unviable works package: in April 2024 the Public Prosecutor's Office said the state had paid certificates for works that did not meet the programme's purpose and alleged a G.180.03 billion patrimonial loss.
