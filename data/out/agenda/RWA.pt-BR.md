## O que os indicadores não veem sobre Ruanda

Entregas documentadas ligadas a indicadores ausentes. Elas não afetam as notas nem a solidez da evidência.

- **Umuganda community work** (Propósito compartilhado). Rwanda requires a morning of community work on the last Saturday of each month, and the government's own assessment reports average participation of 91.3 percent of adults countrywide in fiscal year 2015-2016, up from 87.8 percent two years earlier.
- **Rwanda's adult literacy centres, and their decline** (Aprendizagem). Rwanda's Ministry of Education reports 93,548 learners in adult literacy centres in 2024/25, down from 152,015 in 2017.
- **Government of Rwanda and Zipline drone delivery network** (Coordenação). From 2016 the Government of Rwanda contracted Zipline to fly blood and later other medical products to health facilities, and by 2024 the Rwanda Biomedical Centre reported deliveries to 624 sites across 27 districts and over 12,000 packages in a single month.
- **Rwanda's 2024 Marburg outbreak response** (Adaptação). Rwanda's Ministry of Health declared a Marburg virus disease outbreak on 27 September 2024 and declared it over on 20 December 2024. WHO reported 66 confirmed cases and 15 deaths, a case fatality ratio of 23 percent, with the last confirmed case on 30 October.
