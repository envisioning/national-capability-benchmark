## Com o que a Malásia conta

As condições descrevem com o que um país conta: infraestrutura, acesso, dinheiro, pessoas, matrículas e a própria renda. Cada uma aparece ao lado de uma capacidade e não entra na pontuação, na solidez da evidência nem na tendência. Lidas ao lado da pontuação, permitem ver se o que o país tem se converte no que ele faz. ## Agenda de medição

18 indicadores previstos no modelo ainda não têm base comparável, e cada um reduz a solidez da evidência. Uma lacuna passa a entrar na pontuação quando uma série comparável cobre pelo menos dois países.

| Antecipação | Capacidade governamental de prospecção | Existência, mandato e continuidade de uma função nacional de prospecção estratégica. |

| Agência | Adultos que realizam tarefas digitais | Parcela de adultos que realizaram uma tarefa digital determinada nos últimos três meses, como enviar uma mensagem com arquivo anexo, mover um arquivo entre dispositivos ou usar uma fórmula em planilha, contada tarefa por tarefa e nunca como nível de habilidade. |

## O que a Malásia construiu e nenhum indicador capta

São mudanças institucionais documentadas neste país, registradas como evidência, entre construções e retrocessos. Aparecem ao lado da pontuação e não alteram a pontuação nem a solidez da evidência.

- **MySejahtera and the national COVID-19 immunisation programme** (Adaptação). Malaysia's national COVID-19 immunisation programme had administered 57,119,777 doses by 31 December 2021, while the MySejahtera application was used to collect 974,134 adverse-event responses for vaccine safety monitoring.
- **MRT Putrajaya Line** (Construção). Malaysia completed and opened the 57.7-kilometre MRT Putrajaya Line in two phases, with 36 operational stations serving a corridor of about two million people; full operations began on 16 March 2023.
- **Kuala Lumpur–Singapore high-speed rail, terminated before construction** (Construção). Malaysia allowed the bilateral Kuala Lumpur–Singapore high-speed rail agreement to terminate on 31 December 2020 and paid Singapore SGD102,815,576 in March 2021 for verified costs incurred on the project and for extending its suspension.
- **National flood-warning SMS system** (Adaptação). Malaysia's National Disaster Management Agency (NADMA), water and meteorological agencies and telecommunications companies sent more than five million flood-warning SMS messages during the 2023/24 monsoon season and upgraded 442 transmission-tower sites to keep warnings and communications functioning.
- **JENDELA national digital-network plan** (Coordenação). Malaysia's JENDELA Phase 1 connected 7.74 million premises with fibre by 2022, above its 7.5 million target, and raised 4G coverage in populated areas to 96.92% through a regulator-led programme delivered with telecommunications providers.
- **DuitNow on the Real-time Retail Payments Platform** (Coordenação). Bank Negara Malaysia merged its clearing subsidiary with the banks' payment consortium into PayNet, jointly owned by the central bank and the financial industry, which rolled out the Real-time Retail Payments Platform with DuitNow from December 2018; BNM statistics record 5,438.6 million RPP transactions worth RM3,638.1 billion in 2025, up from 109.3 million in 2019.
- **Bank Negara Malaysia Fintech Regulatory Sandbox** (Experimentação). Bank Negara Malaysia has operated a fintech regulatory sandbox since October 2016 and added an accelerated Green Lane for financial institutions in February 2024; as of June 2026 it reported 139 Standard and Green Lane applications submitted to date and five solutions in live testing.
- **Public-Private Research Network (PPRN) matching grants** (Coordenação). Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education approved 680 Public-Private Research Network projects involving 634 companies by the end of 2022, with a total allocation of RM27.7 million, and 469 of them had closed as successfully completed.
- **Kolej Komuniti part-time and lifelong-learning (Pembelajaran Sepanjang Hayat) courses** (Aprendizagem). Malaysia's public community colleges, run by the Ministry of Higher Education, counted 259,788 part-time students, including participants in Pembelajaran Sepanjang Hayat lifelong-learning courses, in 2024, against 259,810 in 2023, according to the ministry's Statistik Pendidikan Tinggi 2024.
