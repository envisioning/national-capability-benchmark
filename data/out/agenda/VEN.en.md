## What the indicators miss about Venezuela

Documented deliveries linked to missing indicators. They do not affect scores or confidence.

- **Gran Misión Vivienda Venezuela's five-million-home milestone** (Building). Venezuela's Gran Misión Vivienda Venezuela reported delivery of its five-millionth home on 7 June 2024, with the programme saying that community self-construction supplied 75% of its construction in the preceding four years.
- **Programa Ampliado de Inmunizaciones, and its erosion** (Building). WHO and UNICEF estimated Venezuela's national DTP3 coverage among one-year-olds at 50 percent in 2025, down from 87 percent in 2015, and coverage has not returned above 66 percent in any year since 2017.
- **COVID-19 vaccination campaign** (Adaptability). Venezuela's health authorities reported to WHO that 22,157,232 people, 78 per 100 population, had received at least one COVID-19 vaccine dose by 29 December 2023, from a first dose given on 22 February 2021, with 14,287,370 (50 per 100) completing the primary series.
