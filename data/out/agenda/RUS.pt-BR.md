## O que Rússia tem para trabalhar

Condições descrevem o que um país tem para trabalhar: infraestrutura, acesso, dinheiro, pessoas, matrículas e a própria renda. Cada uma aparece ao lado de uma capacidade e não entra na nota, na solidez da evidência nem na tendência. Lidas contra a nota, mostram se o que o país tem se converte no que ele faz. A posição conta os países com valor, do melhor para o pior.

## O que os indicadores não veem sobre Rússia

Entregas documentadas ligadas a indicadores ausentes. Elas não afetam as notas nem a solidez da evidência.

- **Government Resolution 218 complex projects for high-tech production** (Coordenação). Under Government Resolution 218 of 9 April 2010, Russia's Ministry of Science and Higher Education subsidised 41 joint firm-university complex projects in their R&D stage in 2024, from the 14th and 15th selection rounds. Federal funding was 2.708 billion rubles, and firms put in 4.14 billion rubles of their own (156 percent of the budget funds). The ministry reports 161 technologies developed and handed over to production by January 2025.
- **Bank of Russia regulatory sandbox (regulativnaya pesochnitsa)** (Experimentação). The Bank of Russia decided in 2025 to create the legal conditions for introducing 4 digital financial services piloted in its regulatory sandbox, out of 10 pilot applications it considered that year in identification, artificial intelligence, insurance, distributed ledgers and other areas.
- **Federal project Sodeystvie zanyatosti (Employment Promotion) vocational training of adults** (Aprendizagem). Under the federal project Sodeystvie zanyatosti of the national project Demography, which paid for vocational training and further professional education of selected groups of adults, more than 80,000 citizens had started training and 21,125 had completed it by the end of the third quarter of 2024.
- **One-off COVID-19 payments to families with children, 2020** (Adaptação). Russia's government adopted rules on 11 May 2020, the day the President announced it, for a one-off payment of 10,000 rubles per child aged 3 to 16, applied for through the state services portal, and on the first day of payment, 1 June 2020, the Pension Fund paid it for 15.3 million children, more than 150 billion rubles.
- **Faster Payments System (SBP), run by the Bank of Russia with the banks** (Coordenação). The Bank of Russia's Faster Payments System, which commercial banks connect to so that customers can pay any account by phone number or QR code, processed more than 3 billion operations worth 14.4 trillion rubles in 2022 with 213 banks connected, and 18.3 billion operations worth 103 trillion rubles in 2025.
- **Roshydromet modernization: accuracy of hazard warnings to emergency agencies** (Adaptação). Russia's federal hydrometeorological service Roshydromet raised the accuracy of its warnings on hazardous hydrometeorological events delivered to regional emergency agencies from 81 to 83 percent to 91 percent, and of its warnings on natural hazards from 88 to 95.8 percent, between 2014 and the end of 2022 under its second national modernization project.
- **Central Ring Road around Moscow (TsKAD), audited by the Accounts Chamber** (Construção). Russia's Accounts Chamber found in June 2020 that the state company Avtodor's Central Ring Road project, whose first stage was originally to be built in 2014 to 2018 and was now scheduled for 2014 to 2021, had risen in cost by 2.6 billion roubles in 2019 to 315.9 billion roubles, and that launch complexes 1 and 5 were only 49 and 61 percent complete on 1 January 2020.
