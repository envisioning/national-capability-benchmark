## O que os indicadores não veem sobre o México

Entregas documentadas ligadas a indicadores ausentes. Elas não afetam as notas nem a confiança.

- **Seguro Popular, and its abolition** (Construção). Mexico built public health insurance for people outside social security from 2003 and abolished it in 2020, and CONEVAL measured the share of the population without access to health services rising from 16.2 percent in 2018 to 39.1 percent in 2022.
- **Puntos México Conectado, national digital learning centres** (Agência). Mexico's communications ministry operated Puntos México Conectado centres in all 32 states, reporting more than 410,000 enrolled members and over 260,000 course enrolments by June 2017.
- **Jóvenes Construyendo el Futuro, government-workplace training** (Coordenação). Mexico's labour ministry linked young people to more than half a million registered workplaces for on-the-job training, reporting 2.95 million beneficiaries nationwide after five years of Jóvenes Construyendo el Futuro in February 2024.
- **COFEPRIS digital regulatory procedures** (Adaptação). Mexico's medicines and health-products regulator COFEPRIS completed 509,921 procedures entirely online with traceability by 5 September 2024, replacing paper hand-offs for a large share of its regulated users.
- **CFE Telecomunicaciones e Internet para Todos** (Construção). Mexico's state telecom operator CFE Telecomunicaciones e Internet para Todos installed 113,683 free public internet sites by September 2024, reaching 81.2 percent of its stated 140,000-site 2025 goal.
- **Simulacro Nacional 2024, a nationwide emergency exercise** (Adaptação). Mexico's national civil-protection system coordinated a countrywide earthquake exercise in September 2024 in which 9,727,000 people participated, with alerts carried over 14,490 public loudspeakers and a multi-agency task force of 1.9 million personnel.
- **PROSPERA, and its replacement by a new scholarship authority** (Adaptação). Mexico abolished the 2014 decree creating PROSPERA on 31 May 2019 and ordered its people, assets and budget to transfer to the new Benito Juárez scholarship coordination within 90 days.
- **New International Airport of Mexico, and its cancellation** (Construção). Mexico cancelled the partially built Texcoco airport after construction and contracts had begun; the government's 2019 cancellation paper estimated a 100 billion peso cancellation cost, including 60 billion pesos already spent and 40 billion in non-recoverable commitments.
