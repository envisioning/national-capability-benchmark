## What the indicators miss about Papua New Guinea

Documented deliveries linked to missing indicators. They do not affect scores or confidence.

- **Road Maintenance and Rehabilitation Project** (Building). Papua New Guinea's Department of Works restored or maintained 600 km of national roads against a 640 km target under the Road Maintenance and Rehabilitation Project, closing in June 2012 five and a half years after the original December 2006 closing date, with national road works under the original loan costing US$35.62 million, 158 percent of their US$22.52 million appraisal estimate.
- **Lae Port Development Project (Lae Tidal Basin, phase 1)** (Building). Papua New Guinea's Independent Public Business Corporation, using ADB and OPEC Fund loans, built the first phase of the Lae tidal basin, a new berth and container terminal at the port of Lae, between April 2012 and December 2014. It was about four years later than planned and cost $310.4 million, against a $154 million project at the 2007 approval.
