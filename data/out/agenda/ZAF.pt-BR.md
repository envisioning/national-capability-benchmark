## O que os indicadores não veem sobre a África do Sul

Entregas documentadas ligadas a indicadores ausentes. Elas não afetam as notas nem a solidez da evidência.

- **Grid power, and load shedding** (Construção). South Africa ran one of the world's cheapest reliable grids and extended it to most of the population after 1994, then under-invested and lost the surplus, reaching 335 days of load shedding in 2023 before recovering to 83 days in 2024.
- **Labour Market Intelligence Programme and forward skills planning** (Antecipação). South Africa's Department of Higher Education and Training ran a Labour Market Intelligence Programme from 2012, published the fifth iteration of its national occupations-in-high-demand list by April 2024, and identified 138 occupations for the emerging hydrogen-economy value chain.
- **Renewable Energy Independent Power Producer Procurement Programme** (Coordenação). South Africa's IPP Office reported 95 independent power producers operational with 7,335 MW online by 31 March 2025, alongside 9,618 MW procured from 116 projects at financial close and R292.2 billion attracted for energy infrastructure.
- **MeerKAT radio telescope, a delivered national research facility** (Construção). South Africa's National Research Foundation and SARAO completed the 64-dish MeerKAT radio telescope in March 2018 on schedule and on budget, then began science operations as a precursor to the Square Kilometre Array.
- **SETAs and workplace-based learning delivery** (Aprendizagem). South Africa's Sector Education and Training Authorities reported 45,146 learnership completions, 83,578 skills-programme completions and 99,778 workplace-based learning opportunities for the 2024 post-school education and training intake.
- **Gauteng e-tolls, and their cancellation after public resistance** (Adaptação). South Africa operated Gauteng's e-toll system from December 2013 until the government withdrew the toll declaration effective 11 April 2024, ending urban e-toll charging after roughly 10.4 years.
- **Community Education and Training colleges, and their erosion** (Aprendizagem). South Africa's Department of Higher Education and Training merged the public adult learning centres into nine Community Education and Training colleges on 1 April 2015; the colleges enrolled 273,431 people in 2016 and 120,081 in 2023, against a National Development Plan target of one million by 2030.
- **IFWG Regulatory Sandbox** (Experimentação). South Africa's Intergovernmental Fintech Working Group, a joint body of the financial regulators, opened a cross-regulator sandbox in April 2020 that took 54 unique applications and admitted nine sandbox tests from eight firms in its first cohort, then moved to a rolling intake.
- **TIA Technology Stations Programme, and its erosion** (Coordenação). South Africa's Technology Innovation Agency runs a network of university-based Technology Stations that gave 2,907 small firms science, engineering and technology services in 2024/25, against 3,167 at the five-year high in 2021/22, while the programme's budget fell from R105.9 million in 2020 to R93.98 million in 2025 and only 80 percent of the stations were operational.
