## O que a Coreia do Sul construiu e nenhum indicador conta

Estas são mudanças institucionais documentadas em Coreia do Sul que o framework registra como evidência. Elas aparecem ao lado da nota e não mudam a nota nem a solidez da evidência.

- **Economic Planning Board, long-run industrial coordination** (Coordenação). South Korea's Economic Planning Board operated from 1961 to 1994 as the government body responsible for economic development planning and budget planning before its functions moved through later finance ministries.
- **Korea Internet and Security Agency, a consolidated digital authority** (Adaptação). Korea established the Korea Internet and Security Agency in July 2009 by integrating three predecessor organisations, giving the country one public body for internet development, security and international information technology cooperation.
- **MyData, citizen-directed data portability** (Confiança). South Korea's MyData policy gives people a route to request and direct the transfer of personal data between services, with the Personal Information Protection Commission describing the scheme as a way to give people control over data portability after the March 2023 legal amendment.
- **Korean regulatory-sandbox system across eight sectors** (Experimentação). South Korea expanded its regulatory-sandbox system from ICT and industrial-convergence fields in January 2019 to eight sectoral regimes by 2024, adding finance, regulatory-free zones, smart cities, R&D zones, mobility and circular economy testing.
- **Employer and in-work training under Employment Insurance, and its erosion** (Aprendizagem). Korea's Employment Insurance-funded employer and in-work training reached 4,916 thousand training participations in 2018, then fell to 2,410 thousand in 2025 after the Ministry of Employment and Labor withdrew support for statutory common training in 2019, COVID closed classrooms in 2020 and a cap on outsourced remote training took effect in late 2023.
- **Gyeongbu high-speed railway (KTX), and its cost and schedule overrun** (Construção). Korea built the Seoul to Busan high-speed line planned in 1990 at 5,846.2 billion won for opening in early 1999; by 1993 the Ministry of Transportation re-estimated the cost at 12,174.3 billion won, the first phase opened in April 2004 and the whole line opened in 2010.
- **National Assembly Futures Institute** (Antecipação). Korea's National Assembly enacted the National Assembly Future Institute Act on 12 December 2017 and opened the institute on 28 May 2018 with a statutory duty to predict and analyse changes in the future environment and derive mid to long term national development strategies; its English-language research report archive listed 203 reports in 2026.
- **KMA earthquake early warning service** (Adaptação). The Korea Meteorological Administration expanded the national seismic network from 195 stations when its earthquake early warning service started in January 2015 to 550 stations, and by May 2026 issued early warnings within 5 to 10 seconds of first detection.
