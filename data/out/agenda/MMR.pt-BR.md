## O que Mianmar tem para trabalhar

Condições descrevem o que um país tem para trabalhar: infraestrutura, acesso, dinheiro, pessoas, matrículas e a própria renda. Cada uma aparece ao lado de uma capacidade e não entra na nota, na solidez da evidência nem na tendência. Lidas contra a nota, mostram se o que o país tem se converte no que ele faz. ## O que os indicadores não veem sobre Mianmar

Entregas documentadas ligadas a indicadores ausentes. Elas não afetam as notas nem a solidez da evidência.

- **COVID-19 Economic Relief Plan household transfers, and their halt after the 2021 coup** (Adaptação). Under its COVID-19 Economic Relief Plan, Myanmar's government distributed food to about 5.4 million households in April 2020 and cash transfers that had reached about 6 million vulnerable households by November 2020, and the World Bank reported in July 2021 that these transfers had come to a sudden halt after the military takeover of 1 February 2021.
- **Flood and Landslide Emergency Recovery Project, and its slow delivery** (Adaptação). Myanmar's Ministry of Construction and rural roads department had used US$37.2 million of a US$200 million IDA credit approved in July 2016 for recovery from the 2015 floods and landslides, rehabilitating 60 km of rural roads, 10 percent of the target, and starting no national road works in Chin State, when disbursements stopped after the military takeover of February 2021, and the project closed on its original December 2021 date with an Unsatisfactory outcome rating.
