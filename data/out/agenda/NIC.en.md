## What the indicators miss about Nicaragua

Documented deliveries linked to missing indicators. They do not affect scores or confidence.

- **Rural electrification and near-universal grid coverage** (Building). Nicaragua's Ministry of Energy and Mines reported national electricity coverage of 99.575% in December 2024 after public electrification projects brought power to 732,427 homes and 3,853,005 people.
- **SINAPRED evacuation for hurricanes Eta and Iota** (Adaptability). Nicaragua's national disaster system, SINAPRED, evacuated 160,597 people and sheltered 59,964 ahead of hurricanes Eta and Iota in November 2020, when about 3 million people were exposed, and 21 lives were reported lost.
- **Catastrophe risk insurance through CCRIF** (Adaptability). Nicaragua's Ministry of Finance joined the CCRIF regional catastrophe insurance pool in 2015, renewed every year to at least 2021, raised its cyclone cover ahead of the 2020 season, and received five payouts totalling US$32.25 million, including US$30.6 million after hurricanes Eta and Iota.
