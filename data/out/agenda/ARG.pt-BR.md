## O que a Argentina construiu e nenhum indicador conta

Estas são mudanças institucionais documentadas em Argentina que o framework registra como evidência. Elas aparecem ao lado da nota e não mudam a nota nem a solidez da evidência.

- **The Convertibility Plan, and its collapse** (Adaptação). Argentina ended hyperinflation by fixing the peso to the dollar by law in April 1991, taking annual inflation from 4,923 percent in 1989 to 3.9 percent in 1994, and the regime held until it collapsed in the 2001-2002 crisis.
- **Asignación Universal por Hijo child benefit** (Adaptação). Argentina's Asignación Universal por Hijo (AUH) reached 4,083,987 children and adolescents in June 2024, with 2,324,716 adult titulars receiving the benefit through the national social-security administration and its health, vaccination and education conditions.
- **SUBE national electronic fare and subsidy rail** (Adaptação). Argentina's SUBE system distributed more than five million cards and recorded about 6.7 million new cards in 2024, while expanding the network to more than 30,000 recharge points and making SUBE Digital available in every locality.
- **Vaca Muerta unconventional energy build-out** (Coordenação). Argentina's energy ministry reported that Vaca Muerta supplied 54.9% of national oil output and 50.1% of national gas output in December 2024, within a year in which national oil production reached its highest level since 2003 and gas its highest since 2006.
- **ARSAT-3 satellite programme suspended before launch** (Construção). Argentina's government review found that the ARSAT-3 construction programme, approved in 2015 with a 2019 launch plan, was suspended in 2016–17; the unbuilt satellite forced two later orbital-protection operations and left the original nationwide broadband objective undelivered.
- **Atucha II nuclear plant, and its 33-year build** (Construção). Argentina's state nuclear operator connected Atucha II to the grid in June 2014, almost 33 years after construction began in July 1981, and began commercial operation in May 2016, after a build halted in 1994 and restarted in 2006.
- **SINARAME national weather radar network** (Adaptação). The national water resources secretariat contracted the state technology firm INVAP in 2011 to build Argentine weather radars, and by December 2024 the network operated 14 radars feeding an operations centre at the national weather service, after 11 had covered 40 percent of the territory together with three INTA radars in 2023.
