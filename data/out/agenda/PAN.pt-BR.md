## Com o que o Panamá conta

As condições descrevem com o que um país conta: infraestrutura, acesso, dinheiro, pessoas, matrículas e a própria renda. Cada uma aparece ao lado de uma capacidade e não entra na pontuação, na solidez da evidência nem na tendência. Lidas ao lado da pontuação, permitem ver se o que o país tem se converte no que ele faz. A posição conta os países com valor, do melhor para o pior.

## O que o Panamá construiu e nenhum indicador capta

São mudanças institucionais documentadas neste país, registradas como evidência, entre construções e retrocessos. Aparecem ao lado da pontuação e não alteram a pontuação nem a solidez da evidência.

- **Panama Canal expansion and self-financing operations** (Construção). After the third set of locks opened in 2016, the Panama Canal Authority handled 9,944 deep-draft transits and 423 million tonnes of cargo in fiscal year 2024, reporting B/.4.986 billion in total revenue despite climate constraints.
- **Metro de Panamá network operations** (Construção). Panama's public metro network, inaugurated in 2014 and expanded with Line 2, transported 118,351,007 passengers in the twelve-month operating period reported in its 2024 institutional memory.
- **120 a los 65 social pension** (Adaptação). Panama's 120 a los 65 programme paid a non-contributory pension to an average of about 123,000 older people per payment during July 2019–June 2024, with B/.890.835 million programmed over the period and a national beneficiary register used to target eligibility.
- **Canal water-and-transit response to the 2023–24 drought** (Adaptação). During the extreme 2023–24 drought, the Panama Canal Authority adjusted draft and booking rules to preserve water for the canal and the lakes supplying more than half of Panama's population, reducing daily transits from the normal 36 to 22 in December 2023 while keeping the waterway operating.
- **Plan Panamá Solidario, Vale Digital** (Adaptação). Panama's government created the Plan Panamá Solidario digital voucher during the 2020 COVID-19 closures, reaching 90,492 people in its first month (April 2020), 458,662 in May and a peak of 949,343 in November 2020, then tapered it as employment returned and ended it in 2023.
- **INADEH national vocational training** (Aprendizagem). Panama's national vocational training institute, INADEH, restructured by Decree Law 8 of 2006, recorded 156,032 course graduates in 2021, against 90,107 in 2019 and 38,628 in the pandemic year 2020.
