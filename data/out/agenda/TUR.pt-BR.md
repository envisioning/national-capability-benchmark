## O que os indicadores não veem sobre a Turquia

Entregas documentadas ligadas a indicadores ausentes. Elas não afetam as notas nem a solidez da evidência.

- **The 2001 stabilisation, and its unwinding** (Adaptação). Turkey answered its 2001 banking collapse with central bank independence, bank restructuring and fiscal rule, taking inflation from 54.4 percent in 2001 to 8.6 percent in 2004, and after 2018 the same framework was overridden until inflation reached 72.3 percent in 2022.
- **e-Devlet Gateway, a shared public-service front door** (Coordenação). Türkiye's e-Devlet Gateway ended 2024 with 66,753,526 users, 8,309 online services and 1,080 participating institutions, including 127 private providers alongside central government, universities and municipalities.
- **Marmaray, the Bosphorus rail crossing** (Construção). Türkiye opened the Marmaray rail tunnel between Europe and Asia in October 2013 and the transport ministry reported 196 million passenger journeys in 2024, with 1.226 billion cumulative journeys since opening.
- **AFAD earthquake shelter and recovery operation** (Adaptação). After the February 2023 earthquakes, Türkiye's AFAD established 350 tent cities providing shelter for 2.5 million people, moved about 700,000 people into 414 container cities before the first winter and delivered 46,000 permanent homes within the first year.
- **MEB public lifelong-learning network** (Aprendizagem). Türkiye's Ministry of National Education reported 7,909,495 participants in public adult-learning programmes in 2024, including 6,493,700 attending in person and 1,415,795 using the HEMBA online platform; 5,305,688 earned a course certificate.
- **FATİH education technology infrastructure** (Construção). Türkiye's Ministry of National Education reported that the FATİH project had installed 620,000 interactive classroom boards by 2023 and signed a five-year TÜRKSAT service contract covering about 2,000 schools.
- **YEKA RES-1 renewable tender, and its cancellation** (Construção). Türkiye signed the 1,000 MWe YEKA RES-1 wind contract in February 2018 and completed its factory and R&D centre in 2019, but the Energy Ministry records that the contract was cancelled under a 2024 law.
- **TÜBİTAK 1505 university–industry support programme** (Coordenação). TÜBİTAK's 1505 University–Industry Collaboration Support Programme received 116 project applications and began support for 59 projects in 2024, with 52 fellows working across the collaboration pipeline.
- **Kamu-Özel İşbirliği, the national public-private partnership programme** (Coordenação). Turkey's central government signed 277 public-private partnership contracts from 1986 to September 2025, worth US$112.09 billion in September 2025 prices across transport, energy and health, with 262 in operation, including 18 PPP city hospitals holding 29,325 registered beds.
- **DASK compulsory earthquake insurance** (Adaptação). After the 1999 Marmara earthquake, Turkey's government created the Turkish Catastrophe Insurance Pool (DASK) in 2000 to provide compulsory earthquake insurance, and coverage rose from 23 percent of dwellings as reported by the World Bank in January 2011 to 57.7 percent, or 11.6 million insured homes, by October 2025.
