## What the indicators miss about India

Documented deliveries linked to missing indicators. They do not affect scores or confidence.

- **Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana bank accounts** (Building). India opened 590.9 million basic bank accounts through existing banks from 2014, holding 3.17 lakh crore rupees in deposits as of 19 August 2026.
- **Pradhan Mantri Gramin Digital Saksharta Abhiyan** (Agency). India's PMGDISHA rural digital-literacy scheme enrolled 73,571,965 people, trained 63,941,718 and certified 47,836,188 by July 2024 through 20 hours of instruction in five digital-task modules.
- **SWAYAM national online-course platform** (Learning). India's SWAYAM platform offered more than 20,370 courses and recorded more than 65 million enrolments by 12 August 2026 as a national channel for flexible higher-education and skills learning.
- **Unified Payments Interface, the bank-and-app network** (Coordination). NPCI's UPI rail connected 731 live banks and settled 22,716.07 million transactions worth ₹28,92,138.67 crore in June 2026 through participating banks and payment apps.
- **CoWIN national COVID-19 vaccination platform** (Adaptability). India used CoWIN to register and scale COVID-19 vaccination nationally, recording more than 2.2067 billion vaccinations and 1.1093 billion registrations by 21 November 2023.
- **Delhi Commonwealth Games, and the cost-estimate escalation** (Building). India and Delhi hosted the Commonwealth Games in October 2010, but the Comptroller and Auditor General recorded the total estimate rising from ₹1,200 crore in the May 2003 bid to ₹18,532.31 crore by October 2010.
- **Reserve Bank of India regulatory sandbox** (Experimentation). The Reserve Bank of India ran four themed sandbox cohorts between 2020 and 2024, retail payments, cross-border payments, MSME lending and fraud prevention, admitting 28 entities to test and finding 13 products viable in the three cohorts whose exits it published, and keeps the cohorts open on tap.
- **UDAN regional air connectivity scheme, and its lapsed routes** (Coordination). India's Ministry of Civil Aviation paid viability-gap funding to private airlines to fly underserved regional routes from April 2017. Of the 774 routes awarded in the first three rounds, 371 commenced, and only 174 were still flying in March 2023; only 54 of the 112 routes that completed their three-year concession kept operating afterwards.
- **Dedicated Freight Corridors, Eastern and Western** (Building). Indian Railways, through the Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation, built two freight-only corridors approved by Cabinet in 2008 at ₹28,181 crore and reassessed in 2015 at ₹81,459 crore, and by March 2025 had commissioned 2,741 of their 2,843 route kilometres.
- **Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana, the free foodgrain response to COVID-19** (Adaptability). India's Department of Food and Public Distribution, acting on the Garib Kalyan package announced in March 2020, allocated about 121 lakh tonnes of extra free foodgrains to States on 30 March 2020, and States distributed 37.5 lakh tonnes covering about 75 crore beneficiaries in each of April and May 2020.
