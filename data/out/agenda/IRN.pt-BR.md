## Com o que o Irã conta

Cada uma aparece ao lado de uma capacidade e não entra na pontuação, na solidez da evidência nem na tendência. Lidas ao lado da pontuação, permitem ver se o que o país tem se converte no que ele faz. ## O que os indicadores não captam sobre o Irã

Entregas documentadas ligadas a indicadores ausentes. Elas não alteram as pontuações nem a solidez da evidência.

- **Targeted Subsidy Reform cash transfer, and its erosion** (Adaptação). Iran's government replaced universal fuel and bread subsidies with a universal cash transfer of 455,000 rials (about US$40) per person per month from late 2010, then left the transfer unindexed, so that by 2019 its purchasing power had fallen to less than US$6 per person before a top-up was added in late 2019.
- **Earthquake Emergency Recovery after the 2002 Changureh-Avaj earthquake** (Adaptação). After the April 2002 earthquake in Kermanshah and the June 2002 earthquake in Qazvin, Zanjan and Hamedan, Iran's Ministry of Interior project unit and the Housing Foundation repaired or rebuilt about 24,000 damaged houses to seismic standards by June 2009, using government funds and a US$180 million World Bank loan, of which US$174.9 million was disbursed.
- **Tehran Sewerage Project (first phase: interceptors, trunk main and southern treatment plant)** (Construção). The Tehran Sewerage Company built interceptors, trunk mains, tunnels and a wastewater treatment plant under the first phase of the Tehran Sewerage Project at a latest estimated cost of US$359.22 million against US$341.45 million at appraisal in 2000, with the treatment works costing 189 percent of their estimate and the loan closing on 30 June 2008, two years late, before the plant and the eastern trunk main were finished.
