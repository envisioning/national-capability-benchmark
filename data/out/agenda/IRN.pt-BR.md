## O que os indicadores não captam sobre o Irã

Entregas documentadas ligadas a indicadores ausentes. Elas não alteram as pontuações nem a solidez da evidência.

- **Targeted Subsidy Reform cash transfer, and its erosion** (Adaptação). Iran's government replaced universal fuel and bread subsidies with a universal cash transfer of 455,000 rials (about US$40) per person per month from late 2010, then left the transfer unindexed, so that by 2019 its purchasing power had fallen to less than US$6 per person before a top-up was added in late 2019.
- **Earthquake Emergency Recovery after the 2002 Changureh-Avaj earthquake** (Adaptação). After the April 2002 earthquake in Kermanshah and the June 2002 earthquake in Qazvin, Zanjan and Hamedan, Iran's Ministry of Interior project unit and the Housing Foundation repaired or rebuilt about 24,000 damaged houses to seismic standards by June 2009, using government funds and a US$180 million World Bank loan, of which US$174.9 million was disbursed.
- **Tehran Sewerage Project (first phase: interceptors, trunk main and southern treatment plant)** (Construção). The Tehran Sewerage Company built interceptors, trunk mains, tunnels and a wastewater treatment plant under the first phase of the Tehran Sewerage Project at a latest estimated cost of US$359.22 million against US$341.45 million at appraisal in 2000, with the treatment works costing 189 percent of their estimate and the loan closing on 30 June 2008, two years late, before the plant and the eastern trunk main were finished.
- **Mohit-e azmoon-e tanzimgari (regulatory sandbox) of the Central Bank of the Islamic Republic of Iran** (Experimentação). The Central Bank of Iran's regulatory sandbox, run by its subsidiary the National Informatics Corporation as secretariat, received 10 applications in its first call (July to August 2022) and admitted two to testing, rejected seven and saw one withdrawn, and a second call drew five applications, all of which were under review when the secretariat published its one-year report in August 2023.
- **University contracts with society and industry, MSRT monitoring** (Coordenação). Iran's Ministry of Science, Research and Technology recorded more than 8,700 contracts in progress between universities and higher education centres on one side and industry and executive bodies on the other in its 1400 (2021/22) monitoring, worth about 3,106 billion tomans.
- **Technical and Vocational Training Organization public centres** (Aprendizagem). Iran's Technical and Vocational Training Organization trained 376,248 people in its fixed public centres in the Iranian year 1394 (2015/16), up from 322,657 the year before, in industry, services, culture and arts, and agriculture courses, according to the Statistical Centre of Iran yearbook.
- **Center for the Islamic Iranian Model of Progress (Markaz-e Olgu)** (Antecipação). Iran's Center for the Islamic Iranian Model of Progress, set up by decree of the Leader on 2 Khordad 1390 (May 2011), drafted a document that sets the country's desired horizon for the next five decades, which the Leader issued for review on 22 Mehr 1397 (October 2018), and reports that it analysed 5,300 critiques and proposals on that draft and received 3,803 papers over its first fourteen annual conferences.
