## Com o que a Bélgica conta

As condições descrevem com o que um país conta: infraestrutura, acesso, dinheiro, pessoas, matrículas e a própria renda. Cada uma aparece ao lado de uma capacidade e não entra na pontuação, na solidez da evidência nem na tendência. Lidas ao lado da pontuação, permitem ver se o que o país tem se converte no que ele faz. A posição conta os países com valor, do melhor para o pior.

## Agenda de medição

18 indicadores previstos no modelo ainda não têm base comparável, e cada um reduz a solidez da evidência. Uma lacuna passa a entrar na pontuação quando uma série comparável cobre pelo menos dois países.

| Dimensão | Indicador ausente | O que ele mediria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Antecipação | Capacidade governamental de prospecção | Existência, mandato e continuidade de uma função nacional de prospecção estratégica. |
| Antecipação | Compromissos de pesquisa de longo prazo | Novos auxílios públicos de pesquisa concedidos por concurso com duração de cinco anos ou mais, como parcela de todos os auxílios públicos por concurso concedidos no ano. |
| Agência | Adultos que realizam tarefas digitais | Parcela de adultos que realizaram uma tarefa digital determinada nos últimos três meses, como enviar uma mensagem com arquivo anexo, mover um arquivo entre dispositivos ou usar uma fórmula em planilha, contada tarefa por tarefa e nunca como nível de habilidade. |
| Coordenação | Colaboração universidade-empresa | Intensidade da colaboração em pesquisa entre universidades e empresas. |
| Coordenação | Colaboração público-privada | Frequência e escala da execução conjunta, por governo e empresas, de objetivos nacionais. |

## O que os indicadores não captam sobre a Bélgica

Entregas documentadas ligadas a indicadores ausentes. Elas não alteram as pontuações nem a solidez da evidência.

- **Bureau federal du Plan** (Antecipação). Belgium's Federal Planning Bureau, which produces the government's economic forecasts and long-term demographic, ageing, energy and transport outlooks, published 34 publications in 2025 and employed 91 full-time equivalents at the end of the year, down from 96 at the end of 2023, after it stopped replacing departing staff in 2024 to meet savings targets.
- **Chômage temporaire corona, 2020** (Adaptação). Belgium's National Employment Office treated every COVID-19 work stoppage as temporary unemployment for force majeure and paid allowances to 939,532 workers for March 2020 and 1,117,133 for April, when 1,221,412 workers, 35.3 percent of private and contractual employees, were declared temporarily unemployed; almost 1.4 million received at least one payment during 2020.
- **De Kade prison in Antwerp, a federal DBFM contract** (Coordenação). Belgium's federal Buildings Agency had the new 440-place prison de Kade in Antwerp, part of the federal prisons masterplan, designed, built and financed by the consortium Hortus Conclusus under a DBFM contract, with works from 2023 to 2026 at a construction cost of EUR 195.5 million and an annual availability fee of EUR 23.1 million for 25 years of maintenance, after which the prison returns to the federal state free of charge.
- **Federal strategic stock of protective masks, and its dismantling** (Adaptação). The Federal Public Service Public Health held a strategic stock of masks for pandemic influenza in military warehouses in Namur from 2006, sized at 32 million surgical masks when the Bioethics Committee reviewed the pandemic plan in 2009. It destroyed the expired masks in operations in late 2015 and late 2018 without replacing them, so Belgium entered the COVID-19 crisis in early 2020 with no strategic mask stock.
- **BE-Alert** (Adaptação). The National Crisis Centre of the Federal Public Service Interior has run BE-Alert, the national system that lets municipalities and provinces alert the population by SMS, including location-based SMS, and information screens, since 2017. By 1 September 2025, 537 of 565 municipalities, covering 95 percent of Belgian territory, were affiliated, 1,369,659 citizen addresses were registered, and the system had been used 273 times in 2025.
- **Brussels regional express rail network (GEN/RER) infrastructure works, and their stall** (Construção). Belgium's federal state and three regions agreed in 2001 to fund the infrastructure for a regional express rail network around Brussels through the RER Fund, with every work due by the end of 2012. The Court of Audit told the Chamber in February 2017 that the cost of the works charged to the fund had risen by more than 27 percent over 2001 to 2016, that all but one project ran six to 17.5 years late, that the works were largely at a standstill and that full four-tracking of lines 124 and 161 had been postponed with no date.
- **BRUGEL derogation framework for innovative energy projects (projets innovants)** (Experimentação). BRUGEL, the Brussels-Capital energy regulator, granted derogations from market and tariff rules to energy-sharing pilot projects under article 90 of the Brussels electricity ordinance as amended on 23 July 2018, and states that six such pilot projects started from 2020 until the derogation framework ended when the ordinance of 17 March 2022 entered into force.
- **Automated voting, and its withdrawal from Wallonia** (Construção). Belgium extended electronic voting after a 1991 trial in two cantons until 44 percent of the electorate voted on machines in 1999, when 22 percent of polling stations in Wallonia were equipped; the Walloon Parliament abolished automated voting for French-speaking communes by a decree of 9 March 2017, the Constitutional Court rejected a challenge in September 2018, and the FPS Interior now lists the 252 communes in Wallonia as voting on paper while 152 Flemish, all 19 Brussels and the nine German-speaking communes vote electronically.
- **Wallonia's pôles de compétitivité** (Coordenação). The Walloon government labelled 266 collaborative research, development and innovation projects through its six pôles de compétitivité over 24 calls from 2006 to June 2018, each needing at least two firms and two distinct research actors, with forecast budgets of 930 million euros, and 126 of those projects had been completed with final payment by early 2019.
