## O que a Bolívia construiu e nenhum indicador conta

Estas são mudanças institucionais documentadas em Bolívia que o framework registra como evidência. Elas aparecem ao lado da nota e não mudam a nota nem a solidez da evidência.

- **Bono Juana Azurduy maternal and child health transfer** (Adaptação). Bolivia's Bono Juana Azurduy opened its 2024 payment round through 47 authorised financial institutions in all 340 municipalities and six Indigenous territories, tying the transfer to prenatal, delivery and early-childhood health checks; the programme reported 1,525,566 pregnant women and 1,783,936 children reached between 2009 and 2023.
- **Renta Dignidad non-contributory old-age pension** (Adaptação). Bolivia's Renta Dignidad, created by Law 3791 in 2007 and paid since 2008, reached 1,241,440 beneficiaries in 2024 with Bs5.289 billion disbursed through the national social-security administration.
- **Mi Teleférico urban cable-car network** (Construção). Bolivia's Mi Teleférico network carried 78,715,401 passenger trips in 2024; its management report compared that result with a 97.7 million annual volume required for budget balance, or roughly 80% of the target while the system remained in service.
- **Industrial lithium carbonate plant below design capacity** (Construção). Bolivia's state lithium company doubled lithium-carbonate output to 2,064 tonnes in 2024 after its industrial plant began operating, but the Ministry of Economy reported that technical problems with evaporation ponds and the water-treatment plant kept production below the project's projected capacity of 15,000 tonnes a year.
- **El Mutún steel complex, inaugurated in 2025 and found never to have operated** (Construção). Bolivia's Empresa Siderúrgica del Mutún, whose turnkey contract with Sinosteel was awarded in 2016 and whose complex the President inaugurated in February 2025, reported in its 2026 initial accountability review that the project stood at 92% physical progress against 100% accumulated financial execution, that its own supervision found the complex had never entered operation, and that it had served notice of intent to terminate the contract in January 2026.
- **Yo, sí puedo national literacy campaign and post-literacy programme** (Aprendizagem). Bolivia's Ministry of Education reports that its national literacy programmes, run as 'Yo, sí puedo' from 2006 to 2008 and continued as a post-literacy programme from 2009, taught more than one million people over 15 to read and write between 2006 and 2017, 74% of them women.
- **Interoperable QR immediate payments, built by the banks and the Banco Central de Bolivia** (Coordenação). Bolivia's banks first offered QR payments under their own SIMPLE standard, and the Banco Central de Bolivia then made QR transfers interoperable across banks, cooperatives, housing lenders and development finance institutions with a universal standard compatible with the banks' codes, mandatory electronic channels and free transfers; immediate QR payments rose from 6 million operations in 2021 to 892 million in 2025, 86 percent of electronic fund transfer orders that year.
- **COVID-19 vaccination campaign** (Adaptação). Bolivia's health authorities reported to WHO that 7,505,389 people, 64 per 100 population, had received at least one COVID-19 vaccine dose by 29 December 2023, from a first dose given on 30 January 2021, with 6,307,628 (54 per 100) completing the primary series.
