# Shared purpose source sweep, November 2026

Date: 2026-11-02. Dataset 9.4.0, 125 countries (D153). Track: source-backed
measurement, desk triage plus coverage counts. Nothing is wired: no registry
row, adapter, observation or output changed.

## What this sweep tests

Shared purpose asks whether people act together toward common ends beyond
their own household. It is the dimension furthest from O1. The sweep looks for
open sources (D154) that add observed rows across the 125, decided on
construct first (D118), with each candidate's coverage counted by income
quartile so that its effect on the guardrail is known before anything is
wired.

## Candidate that passes everything

### OECD Global Revenue Statistics as fill and refresh for `tax_revenue_gdp`

**Construct.** Unchanged from the scored row: tax collected as a share of GDP,
an outcome read as revealed willingness to fund common goods. GRS observes
the same thing as `GC.TAX.TOTL.GD.ZS` under the OECD classification of taxes.
It is not a new construct and it does not add a row. It reduces one
confound the registry note names: GRS separates central, state, local and
social security sectors, so a fill can match the WDI definition (central
government) instead of guessing it.

**Licence.** OECD terms and conditions, section 3 (Data): "Except where
additional restrictions apply as stated above, you can extract from,
download, copy, adapt, print, distribute, share and embed Data for any
purpose, even for commercial use. You must give appropriate credit to the
OECD". URL: https://www.oecd.org/en/about/terms-conditions.html (read through
the Internet Archive copy of 2026, because oecd.org refuses scripted
requests). The same section says data "may be subject to restrictions beyond
the scope of these Terms and Conditions" where third parties own them. The
dataflow metadata names no such restriction.

**Download.** OECD SDMX API, no key, no account:
`https://sdmx.oecd.org/public/rest/data/OECD.CTP.TPS,DSD_REV_COMP_GLOBAL@DF_RSGLOBAL(2.1),/all?startPeriod=2019&format=csvfilewithlabels`.
CSV, 554,816 rows from 2019. Filter `MEASURE=TAX_REV`, `UNIT_MEASURE=PT_B1GQ`,
`SECTOR=S1311` (central government), `STANDARD_REVENUE=_T` minus `T_2000`
(social security contributions). The database page gives 141 economies and
a last update of 5 May 2026; the API returns 145 reference areas.

**Years and coverage.** 99 of 125, latest year 2024 for 88 and 2023 for 11.
By quartile 20 / 24 / 26 / 28. GRS lacks 26 frame countries, 23 of which WDI
already covers (AGO ALB ARE BDI BIH BLR CYP ETH GNB IND IRQ JOR LBN MKD MMR
NPL RUS SDN SRB TJK TZA UZB ZWE), so GRS cannot replace WDI. As a fill it
supplies 9 of the 12 countries the row now lacks: Bolivia, Cuba, Guinea, the
Gambia, Indonesia, Japan, Nigeria, Pakistan and Vietnam. By quartile that is
3 / 4 / 0 / 1, plus Cuba. Haiti, Iran and Venezuela stay empty. GRS also has
a later year than WDI in 19 countries whose WDI value dates from 2012 to 2023.

**Agreement with the scored row.** On the 83 country-years where both exist,
GRS central government net of social security contributions correlates with
the WDI value at r = 0.922, median difference 0.0 points, median absolute
difference 0.3. The large gaps are sector-allocation cases: France (WDI 22.8,
GRS 12.4) and Sweden (27.2 against 17.4), where taxes WDI books centrally sit
with social security funds or local government in GRS, and Argentina (10.4
against 17.7). A fill rule must state which source wins and log both.

**Effect.** Simulated with the scorer's formula:

| Run | Mean confidence | Q1 / Q2 / Q3 / Q4 | Guardrail |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 9.4.0 | 0.318 | 0.250 / 0.309 / 0.366 / 0.363 | 0.474 |
| GRS fills the 9 empty countries | 0.330 | 0.266 / 0.329 / 0.367 / 0.368 | 0.464 |
| GRS fills the 9 and refreshes the 19 older WDI values | 0.336 | | 0.460 |

O1 moves by about 0.02 and is still missed. The guardrail falls by about
0.014, because 7 of the 8 filled countries with an income figure are in Q1 or
Q2. The income correlation of the values is not computed here and decides
nothing.

**Risks.**

- Third-party ownership. GRS is built with regional partners (the Africa,
  Latin America and Asia-Pacific editions are co-published). The OECD terms
  put the check on the user. The dataflow carries no restriction, but each
  regional edition's copyright page must be read before wiring. If one
  restricts reuse, those countries leave the fill.
- The dataflow carries the annotation `NonProductionDataflow: true`. Its
  meaning for stability is unknown. Pin the dataflow version (2.1) and the
  retrieval date, as D124 does for OpenAlex.
- Mixing two publishers in one row. A refresh changes the year and the
  value of 19 countries. The sector-allocation cases show that "central
  government" is not identical across the two. The safer first step is fill
  only, and refresh only where the two sources agree within a stated
  tolerance on an overlapping year.
- The row's construct problems stay: resource revenue and federal structure.
  Q3 of the roadmap holds `income_inequality`, not this row, for a construct
  audit.

**Next step.** One decision entry: `tax_revenue_gdp` gains a fill-only second
source, OECD GRS central government tax net of social security
contributions, used only where WDI has no value in the recency window, with
the publisher on each observation. Read the regional editions' copyright
pages first. Then an adapter that pins the dataflow version, writes the
observation with `source` set to GRS, and logs every fill in
`revisions.json`.

## Rejected and why

- UNU-WIDER GRD: no licence or redistribution term on the publisher's pages (D154).
- IMF WoRLD and ISORA: commercial reuse needs a request to copyright@imf.org (D154); ISORA was also rejected on construct in O1-CANDIDATES.md.
- WUENIC vaccination coverage: reads the reach of the health system, a condition money buys (D122).
- Road traffic deaths: reads roads, fleet and trauma care; latest year 2019.
- Remittances: kin solidarity inside the household network, scaled by the migrant stock; not beyond the household.
- ILOSTAT volunteer rates: O1-CANDIDATES.md verdict stands (reference periods from one week to 12 months, rich-weighted); the ILO SDMX endpoint returned 403 from this machine, so the 125 count is not made.
- World Risk Poll: terms silent on redistribution (D154); its shared-purpose items are perceptions of neighbours, the near pole that `EVS-WVS-BEHAVIOURAL-ITEMS.md` rejected for G007_18.
- SDG 16.7.2: perception of government responsiveness, the A5 channel; 75 of 125 and weighted to Q4 (8 / 19 / 18 / 29).
- Open Budget Survey: no licence on the publisher's page (D154); it scores state practice, not people.
- GDELT: open, but media-coded event counts read press coverage and grievance; direction undecidable, as for E027.
- Mass Mobilization: protest counts with undecidable direction; ends in 2020.
- Wikimedia geoeditors: no licence stated; bucketed ranges and excluded countries; gated by internet access.
- World Happiness Report 2026: no reuse terms; the generosity factor is a residual on GDP; Gallup stays D10-closed.
- Afrobarometer Round 9: terms still silent on redistribution; D154 stands.
- LAPOP: forbids passing data to third parties (read from search extracts of `vanderbilt.edu/lapop/raw-data.php`, not from the page itself).
- WHO blood safety: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO fails D154, on top of the construct verdict in O1-CANDIDATES.md.
- GODT organ donation and What a Waste recycling: conditions of the transplant and municipal systems; the 125 frame does not change that.

## Ranked shortlist

1. **OECD GRS fill and refresh for `tax_revenue_gdp`.** The only open source
   that passes construct, D154 and the guardrail: +9 countries, 7 of them in
   Q1 or Q2, mean confidence 0.318 to 0.330, guardrail 0.474 to 0.464.
2. **WVS wave 8 (watch).** The only open route found to the 53 countries
   `civic_participation` lacks; not released on 2026-11-02. Count its African
   and South Asian samples on release, before reading any value.
3. **ILOSTAT volunteer rates (watch).** The gap's own construct under an open
   licence. Re-count at 125 from a machine the ILO endpoint accepts; it fills
   the gap only if four-week rates reach half the frame.
4. **World Risk Poll (watch on licence only).** 113 of the 125 in the 2025
   wave (24 / 28 / 29 / 31), Q1 included. It becomes usable only if LRF states a redistribution
   licence on its own page and a later wave asks a behaviour of acting with
   strangers; the 2023 waste-separation item is not one.

No open source in view takes Shared purpose to O1. With the GRS fill the
dimension reads about 0.33. The remaining distance is a full-frame row or a
filled gap, and every candidate for `volunteering_rate` with frame coverage
is closed under D10 or D154.

## Reproduction

All in a scratch directory, nothing committed.

- Starting figures and simulations: per-country `confidenceParts` and
  `observedIndicators` from `data/out/index.json`, log10 of `gdpPerCapita`
  from `diagnostics.income`. A fill adds one observation at recency 1.0 and
  source quality 0.95 with the denominator held at 6; a new row also adds one
  to the denominator.
- GRS: the SDMX request above; `cover.json` holds the latest year per frame
  country.
- WDI series: `api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/<code>?date=2015:2025`;
  licence from `api.worldbank.org/v2/sources/2/series/<code>/metadata`.
- SDG 16.7.2: `unstats.un.org/sdgapi/v1/sdg/Series/Data?seriesCode=IU_DMK_INCL`.
- World Risk Poll: `storage.googleapis.com/wrp_shares/site_live/wrp_data.zip`
  (132 MB, file dated 2026-09-02), data dictionaries only; no values read.
- Licence pages: OECD terms (Internet Archive copy, 2026), GDELT
  `gdeltproject.org/about.html`, IMF `imf.org/en/about/copyright-and-terms`,
  Afrobarometer `afrobarometer.org/data/data-usage-and-access-policy/`, LAPOP
  `vanderbilt.edu/lapop/raw-data.php`, LRF
  `lrfoundation.org.uk/world-risk-poll-data`, WHR
  `worldhappiness.report/data-sharing/`, IBP
  `internationalbudget.org/open-budget-survey/download`, UNU-WIDER
  `wider.unu.edu/about-grd`.
