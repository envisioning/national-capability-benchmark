# Coordination: source sweep on the 125 frame

Status: desk sweep, 2026-11-02. Dataset 9.4.0. Nothing in the registry, the
adapters or the data has moved. Method: `docs/RESEARCH-ROADMAP.md` (O1, the
guardrail, the open-data rule), D118, D122, D153, D154 and D167.

## 1. What this sweep tests

The question: is there an open, republishable source that adds evidence to
Coordination across the 125 countries, so that the dimension reaches mean
confidence 0.40, without a rise in the correlation of mean confidence with
income?

Starting figures, recomputed from `data/out/index.json` and
`data/out/diagnostics.json` (9.4.0):

| Reading | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Coordination mean confidence | 0.361 (125) |
| Observed rows, mean | 2.5 of a denominator of 5 |
| r(Coordination confidence, log GDP per head) | -0.01 (123) |
| Guardrail: r(mean confidence over nine dimensions, log GDP per head) | 0.474 (123), recomputed here |

The roadmap gives the guardrail baseline as 0.526 at 9.0.0. This sweep
recomputes 0.474 on the 9.4.0 output with the same definition (mean of the
nine `confidence` values per country, Pearson r with the log of
`NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD`). This memo reports every guardrail move against 0.474,
the figure on the same output. The difference from 0.526 is a release
difference that this memo did not trace.

Scored rows and their reach, by quartile of log GDP per head (Q1 poorest; 31,
31, 31 and 30 countries; Cuba and Venezuela have no income figure):

| Row | Covers | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | Latest year |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `trade_compliance_efficiency` (`IC.BRE.IT.P3`) | 74 | 16 | 19 | 21 | 18 | 2025 |
| `budget_execution_fidelity` (`GF.XPD.BUDG.ZS`) | 113 | 31 | 30 | 30 | 22 | 2016 to 2024 |
| `civil_society_strength` (V-Dem `v2x_cspart`) | 125 | 31 | 31 | 31 | 30 | 2025 |

Mean Coordination confidence by quartile: Q1 0.354, Q2 0.379, Q3 0.393, Q4
0.336. The rich quartile reads lowest because budget execution misses eight
of its countries. The two declared gaps are `university_industry_collaboration`
and `public_private_collaboration`.

Confidence is coverage × recency × source quality (`confidenceFor`,
`packages/core/src/pipeline/score.ts`). The simulations below use that formula
on the 9.4.0 cells. They are not a rescore.

## 3. Candidates that pass everything

Only one: the existing B-READY row, deepened by the 2026 tranche. It needs no
new source and no registry change.

### B-READY 2026 tranche for `trade_compliance_efficiency`

**Construct.** Unchanged from D167. The third pillar of B-READY International
Trade scores the time and cost firms report to comply with export and import
requirements. Several border agencies act on each shipment, so the time and
cost read whether they act together. Outcome, capability. The series note
also names digital trade participation and perceived obstacles. D167's
overturn clause on their weight stands.

**Licence.** WDI: "License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0",
https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/World-Development-Indicators.
The B-READY dataset entry (https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0066291)
reads "License: Custom License". The custom text was not read: the catalog API
answered HTTP 429 to this session.

**Download.** `https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/IC.BRE.IT.P3?format=json&source=2`,
JSON, the ingest the row already uses.

**Years.** WDI holds one round, 2025, for 74 of 125 (probed 2026-11-02; WDI
last updated 2026-10-08). The World Bank's covered-economies page
(https://www.worldbank.org/en/businessready/about-us/covered-economies) lists
a 2026 tranche of 62 economies. The 2025 report's preface puts the full data
set at the end of 2026. No 2026 release was found on 2026-11-02.

**Coverage.** 36 of the 62 are in the frame, and none of them has the row
today: Albania, Argentina, Australia, Austria, Bolivia, Brazil, Burundi,
Chile, China, Denmark, the Dominican Republic, Egypt, Ethiopia, Finland,
France, Germany, Guatemala, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, India, Japan, Kenya,
Lithuania, Malawi, Mongolia, Mozambique, the Netherlands, Nigeria, Norway,
Panama, Sri Lanka, Switzerland, Thailand, Uganda, Zambia and Zimbabwe. By
quartile they add 10 / 7 / 8 / 11. The row would go from 74 to 110 of 125
(26 / 26 / 29 / 29). Fifteen stay without it: ARE, BLR, CUB, HND, HTI, IRN,
IRQ, LBN, MMR, NIC, RUS, SDN, UKR, VEN, ZAF.

**Expected effect.** Simulated on the 9.4.0 cells with the 36 new values at
2026 and tier `international_organization`, existing values unchanged:

| | Coordination mean confidence | r(Coordination confidence, log GDP) | Guardrail (recomputed basis) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 9.4.0 | 0.361 | -0.01 | 0.474 |
| 2026 tranche in `IT.P3` | 0.415 | -0.04 | 0.483 |

Coordination clears O1 by 0.015. The guardrail rises by about 0.009 from this
row alone. The added countries are spread across all four groups, so the rise
is small. The same tranche also fills `IC.BRE.BE.P3` (Agency) and
`IC.BRE.DR.P3` (Trust). Those moves were not simulated, so the full guardrail
effect is not known.

**Risks.**
- Timing: the tranche is announced, not released, and the WDI load can lag the
  report (the Enterprise Surveys portal leads WDI by rounds, D144).
- Comparability: D167 kept the 2023 scoring table's weights as read. A 2026
  handbook that weights digital trade or perceived obstacles at more than half
  of the pillar triggers D167's overturn.
- Licence: the B-READY catalog entry's custom licence was not read. WDI's
  CC BY 4.0 covers the series as WDI serves it. Read the custom text before
  the next B-READY ingest, under D154.
- A ceiling: 15 countries stay out, among them Russia, Ukraine, Iran and South
  Africa. The row stays under D167's half-frame gate only while a round keeps
  at least two thirds of its 74.

**Next step.** Run `pnpm bench probe --series IC.BRE.IT.P3` after each WDI
update. When it shows more than 74, run the World Bank ingest and the rescore
in one change, with a decision entry that prints O1, the dimension's r and the
guardrail. Read the B-READY custom licence first.

## 4. Rejected and why

- **OpenAlex domestic multi-institution share** (CC0, 115 of 125, r with log
  GDP 0.36, n 113): the values show that it reads publication conventions, not
  joint work. France reads 0.74 because joint CNRS and university units count
  as two institutions on one lab. Indonesia (0.12 on 1.42 million domestic
  works) and Uzbekistan (0.07 on 86,000) read low on large local-journal
  output. r with the log of domestic output is 0.59. Requests:
  `https://api.openalex.org/works?group_by=authorships.institutions.country_code&filter=publication_year:2022-2024,type:article|review,countries_distinct_count:1`
  and the same with `,institutions_distinct_count:>1`, retrieved 2026-11-02
  without a key. The API now names a shared free daily budget for keyless use;
  D124's adapter should watch that.
- **PEFA PI-2 and PI-1**: the 125 frame changes the earlier reason. PEFA
  assesses mostly low and middle income countries, and the frame now holds
  many of them, so the rich-gap argument against it no longer holds alone. It
  still fails D154: pefa.org returned HTTP 403 to the terms page, the batch
  download page and the 2025 PFM Data Tracker, so no licence line could be
  read. PI-1 already reaches the score through SDG 16.6.1.
- **WHO IHR SPAR C2**: a State Party scores its own attainment of listed
  attributes (a focal point exists, a mechanism exists). That is a self-report
  of an arrangement, the reason the earlier memo gave against OECD TFI, and a
  self-report also fails A13. Licence would pass (CC BY 4.0 at
  https://data.who.int/redirect-pages/footer/learn-more/terms-and-conditions:
  "these Datasets are provided to you under a Creative Commons Attribution
  4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)").
- **SDG 11.b.2 / 13.1.3 local DRR strategies**: adoption of a strategy
  document, self-reported. 92 of 125 and spread across income, so coverage is
  not the problem. If the project ever admits adoption measures, this is the
  first to reread.
- **SDG 17.14.1**: mechanisms in place, 45 countries, 36 of them in the top
  half of income.
- **SDG 17.15.1 (GPEDC)**: observes donors, and covers aid recipients only (0
  in Q4).
- **B-READY MC.P3**: mixes three transactions in one score (D167).
- **B-READY BL.P3**: permits; Agency or Building, not Coordination.
- **OCDS**: transparency, a Trust construct; licences vary by publisher.
- **UN E-Government Survey**: adoption; no 2026 edition with per-country
  interoperability items found.
- **MCV dropout**: the DTP dropout reasons apply.
- **Enterprise Surveys all-agency time**: not new; held by D144. WDI shows
  Belgium and Czechia with customs values at both 2024 and 2025. This sweep
  did not verify whether that is two rounds or one fieldwork span.

Outside this dimension: WDI now serves `IC.BRE.US.P3`, Utility Services
pillar 3, at 74 of 125. D167 states that utility services reach the API only
as a topic score. That reason for `electricity_connection_speed` (Building)
having no successor no longer holds. The Building owner should read it.
