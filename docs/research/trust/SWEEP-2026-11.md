# Trust source sweep, November 2026

Status: desk study, 2026-11-02. Dataset 9.4.0, 125 countries (D153).
Nothing in the registry, the adapters or the data has moved.

Read first: `docs/WHY.md`, the roadmap's O1, guardrail and open-data rule,
D118, D122, D153 and D154, every memo in this folder, and
`docs/research/vdem-sweep/TRIAGE.md`.

## 1. What this sweep tests

The question: which open, republishable sources add evidence to Trust across
the 125, so that the dimension reaches O1 (mean confidence 0.40) without
pushing confidence onto the income gradient?

Starting figures, recomputed from `data/out/index.json` and
`data/out/diagnostics.json` (9.4.0) with the scorer's own confidence formula
(coverage times mean recency times mean source tier, `score.ts`):

| Figure | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Trust mean confidence | 0.392 (misses O1 by 0.008) |
| Trust observed rows, mean | 3.7 of 7 counted (5 scored rows, 2 gaps) |
| Trust confidence by income quartile, Q1 (poorest) to Q4 | 0.312, 0.410, 0.428, 0.433 |
| Guardrail, r of mean confidence with log GDP per head, 123 countries | 0.474 recomputed on 9.4.0 |

The roadmap quotes the guardrail at 0.526, set at 9.0.0. The same calculation
on the 9.4.0 output gives 0.474. I did not find what moved it between 9.0.0
and 9.4.0. Every guardrail figure below is on the 9.4.0 basis, and the deltas
are what matter. Someone should reconcile the baseline in the roadmap before
the next decision entry quotes it.

Income groups are quartiles of log GDP per head (PPP) over the 123 countries
with an income figure: Q1 1,067 to 7,933 dollars (31), Q2 8,145 to 18,779
(31), Q3 20,025 to 42,358 (31), Q4 42,577 and up (30). Venezuela and Cuba have
no income figure.

Coverage of the scored rows today: court compliance 125 (31/31/31/30), bribery
incidence 120 (30/30/31/29), B-READY dispute resolution 74 (16/19/21/18), and
the two Joint EVS/WVS items 72 each (8/20/21/22). The Q1 gap in Trust
confidence is the survey items.

A structural point decides the size of every effect below. Coverage divides by
the counted rows, gaps included (D100). A source that fills one of the two
gaps (`institutional_trust`, `court_case_clearance`) keeps the denominator at
7. A new row raises it to 8 and lowers confidence for every country it misses.

## 2. Constructs, written before the values

Written 2026-11-02 before any country value of a candidate was read. One
exception: for the Enterprise Surveys dispute item I read the range (4 to 100,
median 55) to settle its denominator, because the WDI label says "% of firms"
and the questionnaire routing says otherwise. No correlation was computed
before the verdicts in this section were set.

- **B-READY 2026, dispute resolution pillar 3** (`IC.BRE.DR.P3`). Not a new
  source: the third round of the series already scored as
  `dispute_resolution_efficiency` (D167). Observes the time, cost and
  reliability of resolving a commercial dispute and enforcing the decision. An
  outcome of institutional performance. Capability. The construct is already
  accepted; only the coverage changes.
- **Enterprise Surveys, use of third-party dispute resolution**
  (`IC.FRM.CDP.ZS`, ES `disp2`, questionnaire O.2). Among firms that had a
  commercial dispute in the last three years (O.1, "a failure of any party to
  meet the terms or expectations of an agreement"), the share that used
  courts, arbitration, mediation or conciliation to resolve it. Reported
  behaviour. It observes whether firms act on the expectation that a third
  party will enforce the rule against a counterpart. That is the institutional
  half of Trust, revealed by use rather than stated as confidence. Capability:
  no stock is bought; a country with courts on paper can read low. Direction
  is the open question. A low share can mean disputes settled bilaterally
  (trust between the parties) or disputes abandoned (no faith in the third
  party). The routing removes firms with no dispute, so it does not mix in
  dispute incidence.
- **Enterprise Surveys, largest owner is also top manager**
  (`IC.FRM.LOTM.ZS`, ES `wk20`). Share of firms run by their largest owner.
  Behaviour: hiring a non-owner to run the firm requires trusting an agent
  outside the owning family. That is the delegation argument in the
  management literature. But the share is set at least as much by firm size,
  legal form and the size mix of each country's sample, and by family-business
  norms that are not distrust. Organisation, not trust. At most a check.
- **Global Findex 2025, saved using a savings club or a person outside the
  family** (`fin17c`). Reported behaviour: entrusting money to non-kin with no
  legal enforcement. It is cooperation beyond the in-group in the plainest
  form. But its level is set by whether a formal alternative exists: where
  accounts are near universal nobody needs a savings club. So the value reads
  the absence of a condition (`account_ownership`, already beside Agency) as
  much as trust, and "higher is better" cannot hold across the frame. Check,
  not score.
- **Global Findex 2025, no account because of a lack of trust in financial
  institutions** (`fin11d`). Perception, asked only of adults without an
  account, so the base is the unbanked and the item vanishes where everyone
  is banked. Perception of one institution type. Reject on construct.
- **Enterprise Surveys, any commercial dispute** (`disp1`, O.1). Behaviour of
  counterparts: the incidence of breach. Lower is better. Exposure (more
  transactions, more disputes) and recall bias it. Interesting, but see
  licence.
- **Enterprise Surveys and B-READY firm perceptions of courts** (`crime9`,
  `corr12`, `disp3` to `disp6`). Perceptions. Same family as E069_17, which
  D132 held as a check for the A13 trap. Reject on construct.
- **OECD Trust Survey, 2025 round (2026 results).** Trust in national
  government, courts, civil service, and interpersonal trust on 0 to 10.
  Perception. For institutions, the D132 trap is weaker inside democracies,
  but the item is still a perception. Candidate for the `institutional_trust`
  gap on construct.
- **UN SDG 16.3.1, police reporting rate for robbery or assault.** Reported
  behaviour of victims: going to the police. Revealed institutional trust,
  confounded by insurance rules that require a police report in rich
  countries.
- **UN SDG 16.5.1, bribery prevalence among people** (`IU_COR_BRIB`).
  Experience of a bribe request in a public-service contact. Behaviour or
  outcome; the citizen counterpart of `bribery_incidence`. Capability.
- **UN SDG 16.6.2, satisfaction with last public-service experience; 16.7.2,
  decision-making is inclusive; 16.3.3, access to dispute resolution.**
  Perceptions (16.6.2, 16.7.2) and a reported behaviour (16.3.3).
- **Wellcome Global Monitor 2018 and 2020.** Trust in neighbours (in-group,
  the LAPOP rejection applies), in national government (D132 trap), in
  scientists and doctors (trust in professions). Perceptions.
- **Lloyd's Register Foundation World Risk Poll 2019 to 2025.** "How much do
  you think the government of your country cares about you" and the same for
  neighbours. Perceptions; neighbours are the in-group. The workplace
  violence module asks whether the respondent told anyone, which is not a
  report to an authority. Reject on construct.
- **WJP Rule of Law Index, civil justice factor and General Population Poll.**
  Composite of expert and household perceptions. The same kind of composite
  D23 retired from WGI. Reject on construct.
- **Worldwide Governance Indicators, 2026 update.** Perception composites,
  retired by D23. No change of construct.
- **IMF Financial Access Survey.** Counts of accounts, branches and ATMs.
  Stocks that money buys: a condition (D122), and Agency already carries the
  account condition.
- **Civic honesty wallet experiment** (Cohn and others, 2019). Behaviour:
  whether a stranger's lost wallet is returned. The best construct in this
  list. One-off fieldwork, 2013 to 2016, 40 countries.
- **Global Preferences Survey** (Falk and others). Trust item on a scale,
  2012, Gallup fieldwork. Perception.
- **Afrobarometer, Latinobarómetro, Arab Barometer, Asian Barometer, LAPOP,
  ESS, ISSP, LiTS IV, Global Flourishing Study, Gallup World Poll.** The A165
  stem or near variants, covered construct by construct in
  `SURVEY-COVERAGE-FOR-EXPANSION.md`. Rechecked here for licence changes only.
- **WVS wave 8.** The successor to the pinned release. Same constructs.
- **CEPEJ 2026 evaluation cycle (2024 data).** Court clearance, the
  `court_case_clearance` construct. Rechecked for coverage only.

### 4.1 B-READY 2026, dispute resolution (existing row)

**Construct.** Unchanged from D167: court throughput, cost and reliability for
a commercial dispute. Capability.

**Licence.** WDI series metadata: "License_Type: CC BY-4.0", pointing to
https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses#cc-by, which says the licence
"allows users to copy, modify and distribute data in any format for any
purpose, including commercial use".

**Download.** The existing World Bank ingest: `api.worldbank.org/v2`,
`IC.BRE.DR.P3`, JSON. No adapter change.

**Years.** The 2025 round today (74 of 125). The World Bank's covered-economies
page (https://www.worldbank.org/en/businessready/about-us/covered-economies)
lists 62 economies for B-READY 2026. 36 of them are in the 125: ALB ARG AUS AUT
BDI BOL BRA CHE CHL CHN DEU DNK DOM EGY ETH FIN FRA GIN GNB GTM IND JPN KEN LKA
LTU MNG MOZ MWI NGA NLD NOR PAN THA UGA ZMB ZWE. I found no release date on
2026-11-02; the report has not appeared in the API (all 74 values are 2025).

**Coverage by income.** The additions split 10 / 7 / 8 / 11; the row goes to
110 (26 / 26 / 29 / 29). Still missing after 2026: ARE BLR CUB HND HTI IRN IRQ
LBN MMR NIC RUS SDN UKR VEN ZAF.

**Effect.** Trust to about 0.431, past O1 alone. The guardrail rises by about
0.008, because 11 of the 36 additions are in the richest quartile. That is a
finding to print, not a reason to wait (D118).

**Risks.** The 2026 round may rescore the 2024 and 2025 economies under
revised weights; read the methodology note before re-ingest. Brazil enters the
row for the first time, which moves its Trust score.

**Next step.** On release, run the World Bank ingest, read `revisions.json`,
and record the coverage and guardrail move in a short entry under D167.

### 4.2 Enterprise Surveys, use of third-party dispute resolution

**Construct.** Section 2. Behaviour, capability, the institutional half of
Trust revealed by use. It is the closest open measure to what the
`institutional_trust` gap asks, and unlike E069_17 it does not ask anyone to
praise the state to an interviewer.

**Licence.** As 4.1: the WDI metadata for `IC.FRM.CDP.ZS` carries "License_Type:
CC BY-4.0". The same item on the Enterprise Surveys portal has no licence
statement, so read it only from the WDI API (D154's route for D144).

**Download.** `https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/IC.FRM.CDP.ZS?format=json`,
the existing World Bank ingest. Definition: Enterprise Surveys Indicator
Descriptions, `disp2`, first released 2024-07-05
(https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/methodology/Indicator-Description.pdf).

**Years.** 2022 (4), 2023 (33), 2024 (35), 2025 (38): the 2024 questionnaire
rounds, the same fieldwork as `bribery_incidence`.

**Coverage by income.** 110 of 125: 26 / 27 / 28 / 29. Missing: ARE ARG BLR
CUB HND HTI IRN LBN MMR NIC RUS SDN UKR VEN ZAF.

**Effect.** Filling the `institutional_trust` gap: Trust 0.507, the guardrail
down 0.012 to 0.462, and the Q1 quartile from 0.312 to 0.422. As a new row:
Trust 0.444, guardrail 0.465. Either way O1 holds and the guardrail falls.

**Reported correlations** (computed after the verdict, decide nothing): log GDP
per head r 0.17 (n 110); `court_compliance` 0.04; B-READY dispute resolution
-0.09 (74); `bribery_incidence` -0.13; A165 -0.07 (65).

**Risks.**
1. Noise. The base is firms that had a dispute, a minority of each sample, so
   some countries rest on a few dozen firms. The range runs from Mexico 4.0 to
   Bosnia and Herzegovina 100. The near-zero correlation with every
   neighbouring row fits a noisy series as well as an independent one.
2. Direction. A low share can mean bilateral settlement as easily as despair
   of the courts. The item cannot tell which.
3. Family balance. It is a second Enterprise Surveys row in the institutional
   family, read from the same interviews as `bribery_incidence`.
4. Filling the gap redefines `institutional_trust` from stated confidence to
   revealed use. That needs its own decision entry.

**Next step.** Before any wiring: read the number of disputing firms per
country from the Enterprise Surveys country profiles (published standard
errors or base counts, without the microdata login), and set a minimum base;
run the A13 regime test as D132 did. If at least about 90 countries clear the
base and the regime test shows no inversion, write the decision entry: new
row or gap fill, `higher_better`, with the direction caveat in the note.

### 4.3 Global Findex 2025, savings club or non-family person

**Construct.** Section 2. Trust in non-kin shown with money, but its level
reads the absence of formal accounts. Check beside Trust, not scored.

**Licence.** World Bank API metadata for `fin17c`: "License_Type: CC BY-4.0",
https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses#cc-by.

**Download.** `https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/fin17c?source=28&format=json`.
Country ids are ISO3 in the `country.id` field; `countryiso3code` is empty in
this source.

**Years.** 2024 for 77, 2021 for 38, 2017 or 2014 for 7.

**Coverage by income.** 122 of 125 (29 / 31 / 31 / 30). Missing: CUB GNB PNG.

**Effect.** As a check, none on confidence or the guardrail. If someone
scored it against this memo, Trust 0.446 and guardrail 0.467.

**Reported correlations.** Log GDP per head r -0.70 (121). This was computed
after the verdict. The verdict rests on the substitution argument, which
predicts this sign.

**Risks.** A check that reads "high in poor countries" may be taken as a
claim. Its note must say what it reads.

**Next step.** Optional. Wire it as a check only if the Brazil adaptability
report or the Trust page needs an informal-cooperation reading. Not on the O1
path.

### 4.4 Enterprise Surveys, largest owner is also top manager

**Construct.** Section 2: organisation more than trust. Check at most.

**Licence and download.** As 4.2, `IC.FRM.LOTM.ZS`, CC BY-4.0 in WDI metadata.

**Coverage.** 111 of 125 (26 / 28 / 28 / 29), 2020-2025.

**Effect.** As a new row: Trust 0.445, guardrail 0.465. Reported: log GDP r

**Next step.** None unless a firm-size-adjusted version appears.

### 4.5 WVS wave 8 (watch)

Fieldwork 2024-2026. The WVS site still lists wave 7 as the latest download on
2026-11-02, and national teams report data "available in the coming months".
When the joint or WVS release appears, it replaces the pin through the
existing adapter (the earlier memo's recommendation). It is the only route to
the survey items for more poor countries that passes D154 today.

## 5. Rejected and why

- Findex `fin11d`: perception, asked only of the unbanked.
- ES `disp1`: only on the ES portal, which states no licence (D154).
- ES and B-READY firm perceptions of courts: perceptions, D132's trap.
- OECD Trust Survey 2025 round: no Q1 country, guardrail 0.474 to 0.526.
- SDG 16.3.1, 16.6.2, 16.7.2, 16.3.3: UN site terms forbid redistribution; coverage also thin and rich-heavy.
- SDG 16.5.1 people's bribery: most values are Global Corruption Barometer, CC BY-ND 4.0.
- Afrobarometer R8 Q83: terms still silent on redistribution (checked 2026-11-02).
- Latinobarómetro, Arab Barometer, Asian Barometer: D154, no change.
- LAPOP: user agreement forbids passing data to third parties; community trust construct.
- ESS: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 behind a login; Europe only.
- ISSP: GESIS registration.
- Gallup World Poll and Law and Order: proprietary.
- Global Preferences Survey: non-profit and academic use only; 2012.
- Wellcome Global Monitor: open for 2018, but in-group or incumbent perceptions, eight years old.
- World Risk Poll: "care about you" perceptions; licence wording mixed.
- WJP Rule of Law Index: perception composite; licence not readable on the publisher site.
- WGI: perception composites retired by D23.
- IMF FAS: a condition; commercial reuse needs permission.
- Wallet experiment: one-off, 2013-2016, licence not found.
- CEPEJ 2026 cycle: still Council of Europe only.
- LiTS IV, Global Flourishing Study: format and construct, as before.

## 6. Ranked shortlist

1. **B-READY 2026, dispute resolution (existing row).** No new construct
   question, 74 to 110, crosses O1 alone (0.431); guardrail up about 0.008.
2. **Enterprise Surveys `disp2`, use of courts or ADR.** Revealed
   institutional trust, 110 balanced across income, Trust 0.444 to 0.507 and
   the guardrail down; held until a base-size and A13 check.
3. **Findex 2025 savings club, as a check.** 122 countries, open, behaviour;
   its level reads the absence of banks, so never scored.
4. **Enterprise Surveys owner-manager, as a check at most.** 111 countries,
   open; reads firm organisation more than trust.
5. **WVS wave 8, watch.** Not released; the one open route to more survey
   coverage in poor countries.
